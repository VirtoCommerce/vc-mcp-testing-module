Inviting a member creates BOTH a contact and a security account immediately, and the account already carries the role chosen in the invite dialog - the invitation is not a queued message, it is a provisioned account that cannot yet be used. On the storefront roster the row shows the literal name 'Invite sent' with an empty real name, the chosen role, the email lowercased, and status 'Invited' - so the status column does have a third value beyond Active and Blocked. On the Admin account blade the same person shows Locked state = Locked and a Status field that is EMPTY, rendering as the 'Select ...' placeholder. So a pending invitee and a blocked member are both simply 'Locked' in Admin, and the field that could tell them apart is blank for the invitee. Do not read a Locked account as evidence that someone was blocked: check the contact's own status, where a pending invitation reads Invited and a block reads Locked. Completing the registration is what clears the lockout; a deployment with no reachable mailbox cannot move a member out of this state from the storefront side.
