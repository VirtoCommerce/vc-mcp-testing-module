On a store with no tax provider enabled, the discount row's discountAmountWithTax stays 0.0000 while its discountAmount holds the real reduction (34.8990) - it does not fall back to mirroring the untaxed amount. The order-level discountTotalWithTax is populated (34.90) on the same order, so the zero is specific to the row inside CustomerOrder.discounts. Reading the row's WithTax field as the discount will silently report no discount at all.
