The 'Companies and contacts' keyword box is served by the member SEARCH INDEX, while the unfiltered list is served from the database. A member whose index entry is missing or stale is therefore invisible to keyword search and simultaneously present, one screen earlier, in the plain list - and the search returns a clean 'No data' rather than any hint that it looked somewhere else. The tell is on the member's own blade: a widget reading 'Indexed <date>' for indexed members and 'Search index missing' for the others. When a member you can see will not come back from search, stop varying the search phrase - page the unfiltered list, or find the account through Security > Users, which does not go through that index.
