They are the same assignment, read and written through two doors. A role changed through the storefront's Change role dialog (Mutations.changeOrganizationContactRole) appears immediately under Admin > Security > Users > <account> > Roles, and a role changed and SAVED in Admin appears in the storefront's /company/members Role column after the member re-authenticates. The storefront offers only a subset of the platform's roles - here 3 of the ~15 the Admin assign-list shows - because it lists what GET /api/members/customers/invite/roles returns, so a role assigned in Admin that is outside that subset has no representation in the storefront's own dialog. Practical consequence: to give a test member a role, either door works, but the member's live session keeps the role its token was minted with, so re-authenticate before asserting on the new role.
