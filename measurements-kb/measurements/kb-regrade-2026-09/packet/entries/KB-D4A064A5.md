The reward amount and the cart total round differently. DiscountType.amount carries the raw computed reward at full precision (10% of a 299.97 subtotal is reported as 29.997), while CartType.discountTotal and CartType.total are rounded to currency precision (30.00 and 269.97). An assertion that compares DiscountType.amount against the displayed discount will fail on any subtotal whose percentage is not exact to two decimals.
