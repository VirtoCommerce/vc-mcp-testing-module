## Live defect found — not an oracle item

`POST /api/catalog/search/products` returns **500** on `skip:-1`, `take:-5`, or out-of-range `skip`, and **502** on `take:10000`, leaking the raw Elasticsearch exception **including the live index name** to any authenticated caller. Cause: `ElasticSearchRequestBuilder.BuildRequest()` maps `Skip`/`Take` straight onto ES `from`/`size` with no clamping. Suite `049`'s `API-027`–`API-030` (Draft) would fail exactly this way. **File via `/qa-bug`.** The corresponding invariant was deliberately withheld — an invariant that live-contradicts reality is a false oracle.
