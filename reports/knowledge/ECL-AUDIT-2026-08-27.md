## Two tooling defects (code, not oracle — reported, not patched)

1. **Business value is unreachable for 45 of 54 sections.** `eclBusinessValue` reads *only* the `BL Invariant` column (`oracle-significance.ts:315`), which exists in chapter 14 alone; chapters 1–13/15 use the 5-column generic shape. **Appendix D already declares a real invariant for 34 of them.** *Demonstrated:* correcting Appendix D's 15.1 row to `BL-A11Y-001/002/004` did **not** move its label — still `undeclared`. So §9's promotion rule is unreachable for 83% of the library, and no generic-chapter section can ever grow.
2. **A fenced template row is counted as real.** Appendix A's code-fenced placeholder is attributed to the preceding section: **13.3 scores 4 rows and 4/4 `[OBSERVED]` when it has 3** — the sole remaining `unresolved`. Fix: strip fenced blocks before row extraction.
