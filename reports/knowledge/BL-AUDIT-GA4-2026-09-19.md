## What this settles — the two facts from SMK-015 (P0-revenue mis-triage risk)

1. **`value` = order subtotal excluding tax/shipping is BY DESIGN**, confirmed by the doc's own
   words and by source (`order.subTotal.amount`, with `shipping`/`tax` reported as separate fields).
   SMK-015's framing ("$89.99 sent where the order charged $287.99") is not a `BL-CROSS-005`
   violation — it is the documented contract. **Recommend re-triaging SMK-015**, not filing it as a
   GA4 revenue defect.
2. **`transaction_id` = `order.id`** (the opaque server id), never the order number — confirmed by
   source, and it is what `BL-GA4-003` now states. This makes `GA-030`'s "any non-empty
   server-assigned identifier" the correct assertion and puts `SMK-015`/`040b`/`072c`'s reported
   strict order-number match in conflict with the now-documented contract — **route those three to
   `/qa-review-tests --triangulate` on suite 043** (not this skill's job to edit the CSV).
