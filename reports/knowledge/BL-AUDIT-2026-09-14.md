## The finding of the audit — BL-LOY-018

Measured live on one multi-organization buyer: **156 mission-progress rows** (52 user-scope, 52 per
organization), ~20 of the same missions `Completed` in each, and **19 missions granted in more than one
scope — 38 grants beyond the first, 17,832 points**.

**Two of the three scopes were reached with no administrative action.** Two orders ten seconds apart
under one store mode, one per organization: the buyer simply switched company. `ResolveOwnerId` keys on
the **order's** organization, so the owner moves with an ordinary B2B action, not only with an admin
toggle. Within each scope the invariant holds perfectly — zero duplicates.

This materially widens **VCST-5954**, which frames the defect as an adoption-time migration event.
The re-grant is reachable by any multi-organization buyer, unaided.
