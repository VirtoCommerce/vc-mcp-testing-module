## Applied

**`BL-LOY-015`** — *A subsystem that settles many entities per event MUST expose per-entity attribution of the settlement.* `[P0-revenue]`, status **VIOLATED**.

- **Docs:** `N/A` per §1a (project-specific extension — Loyalty Missions ships in PR #14 with no VirtoOZ surface; checked, absent).
- **Source:** `LoyaltyMissionTransaction(MissionId, ObjectId, UserId)` exists behind a composite index and drives the accrual dedup (`LoyaltyMissionLogicService.cs:311`, `:488-497`) — and nothing exposes it.
- **Live:** full schema introspection, vcst-qa, 2026-08-28. The loyalty surface is three root queries; `LoyaltyOperationLogObject` exposes only `type` / `orderId` / `orderNumber`; the schema's only `missionId` is on `LoyaltyUserMission`, the progress record, not the grant.
- **Measured consequence** (`REG-2026-08-28-1154`): one order granted `+250 / +200 / +100 / +0` across four missions — the balance moved 1058 while the mission under test contributed 508.

It was phrased as a general auditability invariant rather than a missions defect, because the failure shape belongs to any handler that fans one event across independent entities into a shared ledger. It is also the entry that **explains why the other five are hard to test**: with no per-entity attribution, every assertion about one entity's contribution degrades to an aggregate.
