## The judgment this run turned on — why `docs: absent` is UNGROUNDED, not §1a `N/A`

§1a lets Source + Live alone reach CONFIRMED when the behaviour is *genuinely undocumentable* — but it
also says **`N/A` never applies to a user-facing behavior a guide would normally describe**, that "no doc
found this session" is a *missing* axis rather than `N/A`, and **"when in doubt, UNGROUNDED, not N/A."**

UCP is absent from every published guide (domain map D8, VirtoOZ queried first-hand 2026-09-17) **because
it has not shipped** — not because it is undocumentable. A buyer-facing checkout handoff, and the
"this link expired or was already used" message a buyer reads, are exactly what a StorefrontUserGuide
narrates once a feature exists. So this is a missing axis, and §1a's tie-break governs.

UCP-002/003/004 sit closer to §1a class 1 (implementation/security mechanics a guide would not narrate)
and are the ones most likely to qualify for `N/A` after merge. That call is deliberate and human, not a
default — recorded in the proposals file's promotion trigger.
