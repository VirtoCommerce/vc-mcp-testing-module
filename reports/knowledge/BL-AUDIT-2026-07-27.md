## Applied (see oracle for full text; summary only)

- **BL-CART-001:** corrected the 2026-07-22 amendment's framing — the backend does **not** refuse to
  persist an out-of-range quantity on either the `addItem` or `changeCartItemQuantity` mutation; both
  handlers save unconditionally. Split the Rule into PDP (client-side pre-commit block, no line added) vs
  cart-line (accept, persist, flag inline, block "Place order") behavior. Independently re-verified live,
  including cart-line persistence across a full page reload.
- **BL-CART-008:** added a coupon-persistence-and-re-pricing clause — a coupon applied on either side of
  a guest↔authenticated merge survives the merge and its discount re-evaluates against the post-merge
  subtotal (not frozen at the pre-merge amount), because every merge unconditionally recalculates before
  saving.
