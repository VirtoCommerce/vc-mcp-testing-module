VCST-5933 · fix verification · 2026-09-23

A locked member can no longer read the organization's order history

VERIFIED WITH NOTES
ModuleVirtoCommerce.XOrder 3.1013.0-pr-52-a3c9PRvc-module-x-order#52 (open)Platform3.1072.0-pr-3108-b6efCustomer3.1025.0Envvcst-qa

The bug

organizationOrders(organizationId:) authorized through CanAccessOrderAuthorizationHandler, which checked only Contact.Organizations (raw membership) and never the lock. organization(id:) re-checked the lock, so the two reads disagreed. The fix now also checks the live OrganizationMembership record and denies when IsCurrentlyLocked is true.
