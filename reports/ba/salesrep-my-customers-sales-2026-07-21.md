# See Your Entire Customer Portfolio From One Dashboard

B2B sales reps don't just need CRM access — they need to know, at a glance, which of their accounts are active, which just ordered, and which need a follow-up call today. Virto Commerce's **Sales Rep Hub** puts that view directly in the storefront the rep already works in, no separate tool, no manual tracking spreadsheet.

## Multi-Account Management, Built Into the Storefront

Reps who cover a book of business — a distributor's territory, a channel partner's dealer network, a national account team's regional buyers — lose time each day just figuring out *who to call next*. Without a consolidated view, that means logging into the back office, running order reports, or keeping a personal spreadsheet current by hand.

The Sales Rep Hub gives every rep a dedicated **My customers** view the moment they sign in: every organization they're responsible for, in one table, with the deal-relevant fact that matters most — when that customer last ordered through them, and a direct link into the order itself.

With Virto, you can:
- See your full customer portfolio from a single dashboard, no separate reporting tool
- Jump straight from a customer's row into their most recent order, in one click
- Search and sort your book of business by organization name instead of paging through unrelated accounts
- Know at a glance which accounts have gone quiet (no recent order recorded) and which are active

## Use Cases

- A regional sales rep managing dozens of dealer accounts opens **My customers** each morning to see who ordered overnight before their first call.
- Ahead of a check-in call, a rep pulls up a customer's last order in two clicks instead of searching platform-wide order history for the right account.
- A rep covering a large territory uses the search box to jump straight to one customer by name instead of scrolling a long account list.
- A sales manager onboarding a new rep assigns them a starter set of accounts and confirms, from the rep's own view, that the right customers now show up.

## Strategic Benefits

- **Scalable account coverage:** One rep can track a large book of customers from a single screen instead of juggling reports or spreadsheets per account.
- **Sales agility:** Reps get to the information they need — who they serve, what was last ordered — without waiting on IT or the back office to pull a report.
- **Faster follow-up:** A direct link from customer to last order shortens the path from "who do I need to call" to "here's their order" to seconds.
