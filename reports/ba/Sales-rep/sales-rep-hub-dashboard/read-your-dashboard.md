# Read your Sales Rep hub dashboard

### Introduction

Your **Sales Rep hub** dashboard gives you a quick read on your book of business: how many orders are new, how much your customers have bought this week/month/year, who your best-selling products are, and which of your customers are active. Everything on the page is a summary you look at — none of it needs any setup from you.

![The Sales Rep hub dashboard populated with a stats row and both widgets.](screenshots/doc-dashboard-overview.png)
*The dashboard with real activity: the stats row across the top, Recent orders and Top sellers below.*

!!! note "Who sees this?"
    The Sales Rep hub is available only to signed-in users assigned as a sales representative. Regular buyers don't see it.

Want to rearrange which cards and widgets appear where? That's covered in [Customize your Sales Rep hub layout](../sales-rep-customize-dashboard-layout/customize-your-layout.md) — this guide is only about reading what's already there.

### Prerequisites

- You are signed in with a storefront account that has the **sales representative** role.

### Getting to your dashboard

The Sales Rep hub lives in the **left sidebar**, on any account or company page — not in the account menu in the top-right corner. That top-right menu only has your name, an organization switcher, and **Logout**.

In the sidebar, **Sales Rep hub** is its own section, above **Purchasing**, **Marketing**, **Corporate**, and **User**. Under it you'll find **Dashboard** and **My customers**, with a count next to **My customers** showing how many you have.

!!! tip "Two links are both called 'Dashboard'"
    There are **two** sidebar links labelled **Dashboard**, and the top navigation bar has a third link that goes to yet another one. Only the **Dashboard** link under **Sales Rep hub** is the page this guide describes — it shows your sales figures. The **Dashboard** link under **Purchasing**, and the top-nav **Dashboard**, both open your own personal purchasing dashboard instead. If the numbers you're seeing don't look like sales figures, check the sidebar section you clicked from.

### Understanding the stats row

Across the top of the dashboard are six cards, always in this order:

| Card | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| **New orders** | How many orders are still waiting on attention, with their total value and how many of them came in today. |
| **Active carts** | How many of your customers' carts are open right now, with their total value and how many were started this week. |
| **Orders placed · WEEK** | Total value of orders placed so far this week. |
| **Orders placed · MTD** | Total value of orders placed so far this month. |
| **Orders placed · YTD** | Total value of orders placed so far this year. |
| **My customers** | How many of your customers ordered this month, and how many are new customers. |

These are figures to read, not buttons — none of the six cards is clickable, and none of them shows a percentage change or a trend arrow against a previous period. If you want to compare periods yourself, the WEEK/MTD/YTD figures are already broken out separately for exactly that.

*The stats row: New orders, Active carts, and orders placed this week, month, and year, plus your customer count.*

### Recent orders

Below the stats row, **Recent orders** lists your customers' most recent orders: order number, customer, date, status, and total. Click an order number to open it.

Above the list is a row of status filter buttons (All, plus individual statuses like Cancelled or Completed) — click one to narrow the list to just that status. Click **All orders** in the widget header to see your full order list.

![The Recent orders widget with the Completed filter selected, showing no matching orders.](screenshots/doc-recent-orders.png)
*With a status filter selected and no orders in that status, the widget explains that the filter — not a lack of orders — is why the list is empty.*

!!! note
    If a filter excludes everything, the widget tells you so directly rather than just showing a blank list — so you always know whether there's truly nothing there, or you're just looking at a narrow filter.

### Top sellers

**Top sellers** ranks your top 5 best-selling products, with each row showing the product (name, code, and sometimes a thumbnail), units sold, and revenue. Click a product name to open its product page.

Above the table is a row of category chips you can scroll through sideways, starting with **All categories** — click one to see your top sellers within just that category. Some category names may look repeated in the row; those are separate catalog categories that happen to share a display name.

![The Top sellers widget with the category chip row and a ranked product list.](screenshots/doc-top-sellers.png)
*Top sellers, filtered to a category, with Units and Revenue columns.*

!!! tip "Two ways to see who's selling"
    **Units** and **Revenue** aren't the same ranking read two ways — they're genuinely different lists. Your highest-volume product (most units sold) is often not your highest-earning one, and switching between the two column headers can bring entirely different products into the top 5. Check both if you want the full picture: Units tells you what moves fastest, Revenue tells you what earns the most. The same applies to **Recent orders**, where switching between **Date** and **Total** can surface different orders than the one you were just looking at. Clicking the same column again doesn't reverse anything — each column is its own ranking, not a toggle.

If a period has no sales at all, the widget shows **"No sales in this period."**

### How fresh are these numbers?

!!! note "A few minutes of lag is normal"
    The dashboard figures are refreshed periodically rather than instantly, so an order placed moments ago may take a few minutes to show up. Your administrator controls exactly how often. Nothing on the page shows you a timestamp or a manual refresh button — if a number looks out of date, just check back shortly.

### On a mobile device

On a phone, the sidebar (and the Sales Rep hub links inside it) moves behind the main menu button, so there's nothing on the dashboard page itself telling you you're in the hub rather than your personal account — get there the same way, through the sidebar menu.

Once you're there, all six stat cards stack into a single column, and both **Recent orders** and **Top sellers** drop their column headers in favor of stacked cards per row — in Top sellers, units and revenue are shown together on one line under the product code.

![The Sales Rep hub dashboard on a narrow phone screen, with everything stacked vertically.](screenshots/doc-mobile-dashboard.png)
*The dashboard on a phone: stat cards and widget rows stacked into one column.*

### If you have no activity yet

A newly assigned rep, or one with no served customers yet, sees the same layout with empty states in place of data — there's nothing broken about an empty dashboard.

![The Sales Rep hub dashboard for a rep with no orders or sales activity yet.](screenshots/doc-empty-dashboard.png)
*An empty dashboard: the stat cards show zero figures and both widgets display their empty-state messages.*
