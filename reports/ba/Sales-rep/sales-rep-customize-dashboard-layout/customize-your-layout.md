# Customize your Sales Rep hub layout

### Introduction

Your **Sales Rep hub** dashboard and your customer profile pages arrive in a standard arrangement, but you can rearrange them to match how you actually work: move the figures you check first to the front, move the widgets you use most to the top, and hide the ones you never look at. Your arrangement is saved to your account, so it follows you to every browser and device you sign in from.

![The Sales Rep hub dashboard in its standard arrangement, with the stats row across the top and the Recent orders and Top sellers widgets below.](screenshots/doc-hub-dashboard-default.png)
*The dashboard as it looks before you change anything: six stat cards across the top, then your widgets.*

!!! note "Who can do this?"
    Layout editing is part of the **Sales Rep hub**, so it is available only to signed-in users who are assigned as a sales representative. Regular buyers do not see the hub or the **Edit layout** button.

### Prerequisites

- You are signed in with a storefront account that has the **sales representative** role.
- The **Sales Rep hub** section appears in your account menu.

!!! tip "Two different dashboards"
    The **Dashboard** link inside **Sales Rep hub** (`/company/dashboard`) is your sales dashboard. The **Dashboard** link in the top navigation is your own personal purchasing dashboard — a different page. This guide is about the Sales Rep hub one.

### Enter edit mode

1. In the account menu, under **Sales Rep hub**, click **Dashboard**.
2. Scroll to the bottom of the dashboard and click **Edit layout**.
3. The edit bar appears above the page content and the button changes to **Editing layout**. The bar explains both ways to rearrange things and offers **Reset**, **Cancel**, and **Save layout**. The stats row is now labelled **Visible stats**.

   ![The edit bar showing the drag and keyboard hints alongside the Reset, Cancel and Save layout buttons.](screenshots/doc-edit-bar.png)
   *In edit mode you get a drag hint, a keyboard hint, and the three controls.*

!!! note
    Entering edit mode changes nothing on its own. Nothing you do here is kept until you click **Save layout**.

### Move things around with the mouse

**Stat cards** (the row of figures at the top) — grip a card **anywhere on the card** and drag it left or right to its new position.

**Widgets** (Recent orders, Top sellers, and on a customer profile also Quick actions and Customer information) — grip a widget by the **handle in its header**, not by its body, and drag it up or down.

![The Top sellers widget header in edit mode, showing the drag handle and the x control.](screenshots/doc-widget-hide-control.png)
*Each widget header gains a drag handle (Reorder) and an ✕ (Hide) control in edit mode.*

!!! note "Columns are separate"
    On a customer profile, the main column and the right-hand rail are separate groups. You can reorder within each one, but you cannot drag a widget from one column into the other.

### Move things around with the keyboard

Everything above also works without a mouse:

1. **Tab** to a card's or widget's drag handle.
2. Press **Space** to pick it up. The page announces the block's name and its position, for example *"New orders grabbed. Position 1 of 6."*
3. Press the **arrow keys** to move it — left/right in the stats row, up/down in a widget column.
4. Press **Space** to drop it, or **Escape** to cancel and put it back where it was.

In the stats row, **Up** and **Down** hide and show the card instead of moving it.

### Hide what you don't use

**A widget** — click the **✕** in its header. It moves into a **Hidden widgets** tray at the bottom of the page. To bring it back, click its pill in the tray.

![The Hidden widgets tray with a restore pill for Top sellers.](screenshots/doc-hidden-tray.png)
*Hidden widgets are never lost — they wait in the tray until you restore them.*

**A stat card** — drag it from the **Visible stats** row down into the **Hidden stats** zone (or pick it up with **Space** and press **Down**). Drag it back out — or press **Space** then **Up** — to show it again. While the zone is empty it prompts you with *"Drag a stat here to hide it"*.

![The Hidden stats zone holding the Active carts card, with the remaining five cards in the Visible stats row above.](screenshots/doc-parked-zone.png)
*Cards you park in the Hidden stats zone disappear from the dashboard once you save.*

!!! tip
    The stats row closes up around a hidden card, so hiding one never leaves a gap.

### Save, discard, or start over

| You want to… | Click | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Keep your arrangement | **Save layout** | The edit bar closes and your arrangement is stored on your account. |
| Throw away this session's changes | **Cancel** (or **Editing layout**) | Edit mode closes and the page returns to your last saved arrangement. |
| Go back to the standard arrangement | **Reset**, then **Save layout** | **Reset** restores the standard order and shows every block again — but only as a draft. Without **Save layout** your previous arrangement stays in place. |

!!! warning
    **Reset** on its own does not undo a saved layout. If you leave the page or reload after **Reset** without saving, your earlier arrangement is still there.

### Where your layout applies

- Your saved layout follows **your account**, not the browser. Sign in on a second computer or phone and you see the same arrangement.
- The **dashboard** and the **customer profile** pages have their own separate arrangements.
- A customer profile layout is **shared across all your customers** — arrange one profile and every customer you serve opens the same way. It is not saved per customer.

### On a mobile device

Editing works the same on a phone. The stats row, the widgets, and the edit bar stack into a single column so everything stays reachable.

![The dashboard in edit mode on a narrow phone screen, with everything stacked into one column.](screenshots/doc-mobile-edit.png)
*Edit mode on a 390 px-wide screen.*

### Troubleshooting

- **There is no Edit layout button** — your saved layout could not be loaded. A message at the top of the page explains this and the page falls back to the standard arrangement. Editing stays switched off on purpose so a saved arrangement can't be overwritten by mistake. Reload the page and try again.
- **"Your layout couldn't be saved"** — the save didn't reach the server. Your changes are still on screen and you are still in edit mode, so click **Save layout** again.
- **My arrangement is gone after I clicked Reset** — **Reset** replaces the draft with the standard arrangement. If you saved after that, the old arrangement is gone; rearrange and save again.
- **A widget disappeared and I don't know why** — check the **Hidden widgets** tray in edit mode, or the **Hidden stats** zone for a missing figure.
- **I can't drag a widget** — grip it by the handle in its **header**. Dragging from the widget body does nothing on purpose, so you can still select text inside it.
