## 2. H-a evidence — `usageCount` reconciled against ground truth (all 107 pages)

| Component | `usageCount` | Pages truly holding a `componentRef` | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| `AGENT-TEST-SC-q5` | 3 | `AGENT-TEST-q5-use1`, `AGENT-TEST-q5-use2`, `newSasha` | **MATCH** |
| `AGENT-TEST-SC-vcpt2-trio` | 2 | `AGENT-TEST-vcpt-shared-component`, `newSasha` | **MATCH** |
| `saA1` | 1 | `newSasha` | **MATCH** |

The master case is directly present: `trio` was created `2026-08-19T13:16:21`; its page `AGENT-TEST-vcpt-shared-component` was created **43 min earlier** (`12:33:22`) — that is the originating page, it still holds the placement the create left behind, and **it is counted**.

**Why the 2026-08-19 finding looked like an off-by-one.** `saA1` (created `13:36:55`) lists only `newSasha`, which did not exist until 2026-09-16 — its originating page from that session is absent. But the sweep shows `Sasha1`/`sasha2` hold **no markers at all** today: the placements were removed and usage correctly decremented. The counter is right; the original observation was against a state we cannot reconstruct.

> **The *correct* answer is a product decision this lane cannot rule on.** Established: `usageCount` = distinct saved pages containing a marker, master included, reconciling exactly. Whether the master *should* be distinguished ("used on 2 other pages" vs "3 pages") is an open **UX question for the PO** — currently no surface distinguishes it — and is not a test oracle.

## 6. Risk Areas

- **The Designer↔preview `postMessage` seam is the least-asserted and most error-producing surface here** — throws on every load, nothing asserts it, and it is the most plausible mechanism behind the only open Preview finding.
- **The permission matrix is specified per *resource* but the API is shaped per *sub-resource*** (net-new #5); an AC8 case asserting only the detail GET buys no protection.
- **ZERO invariant oracles still** — no `BL-CMS-*` family; every judgement here is `{SPEC}`/`{OBSERVED}`. Three candidates proposed above.
- **Fixture quality dominates false positives.** Two sessions have now been misled by REST-authored `"Text"` fixtures; until valid lowercase-type fixtures exist, no visual Preview/storefront claim about this feature is trustworthy.
