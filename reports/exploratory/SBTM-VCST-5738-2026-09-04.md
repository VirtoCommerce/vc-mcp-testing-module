## The load-bearing finding: G1/G2/G3 are NOT verifiable on the sanctioned lane

The PR's central claims — *merge*, *dedupe*, *at most one in flight* — need two clicks inside one 1000 ms
window. That is unreachable here for **two independent reasons**, and the second is new:

1. **Tool round-trip latency exceeds the window.** The PR author documents exactly this and fell back to
   unit tests: *"tool round-trip latency between simulated clicks exceeded 1s, so each fired its own
   independent, correctly-handled request."* Confirmed — each `browser_click` here is a separate round trip.
2. **The repo's own hook forbids the workaround.** Firing both clicks inside one `browser_evaluate` was
   **BLOCKED** by `hooks/enforce-real-user.mjs` (§Browser Interaction, `feedback_real_user_interaction`),
   and its `@allow-eval` exception explicitly excludes test runs.

**This is a coverage limit to state, not a gap to paper over.** G1/G2/G3 rest on the PR's 3 new unit tests
(+38 pre-existing in that file) **plus** the deployed-bundle evidence that the config is actually live
(`targets:[…"UpdateShortCartItemQuantity"…,…"RemoveCartItems"…]` and the exact `new Set([...])` merge in
`/assets/index-Nzn__LiE.js`). No browser-lane assertion can add to that, so Artifact A must not pretend
otherwise — a case claiming to verify the merge live would be unfalsifiable on this lane.
Routed to authoring: scenarios 2, 3, 15, 16 are **re-oracled** from `{OBSERVED}` to
`{UNIT-TEST} + {DEPLOYED-BUNDLE}`, and 4/5 (the shopper-side window) stay `{OBSERVED}` but are reachable
only by a human, so they are authored **`Manual`**, not `Draft`.
