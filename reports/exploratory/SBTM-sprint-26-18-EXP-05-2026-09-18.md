## Oracle Feedback

All four rows are `Oracle ref: NONE`. Two proposals for `/qa-review-oracles` (not applied here — oracle files untouched):

1. **BL-PAY-004 amendment (mechanism, not label):** the rule describes `allowCartPayment` rendering inline on `/cart`, but says nothing about the **prerequisite order** — a method cannot be *retained* before a shipping address exists. NN-3 shows the retention gate exists and is silent. Suggest adding a prerequisite clause + a "never a silent revert" violation signal.
2. **New candidate invariant — module arrival vs. module behaviour:** a storefront module gated by store module settings must fail *closed and quietly* (no route, no menu entry, no payment option, no console error) and must never leave a half-registered extension point. Grounded 2-of-3 here (source + live); needs a docs axis before promotion.

## Risk Areas

- **Late-chunk dependency (NN-2)** is the live residue of VCST-4386 and is untested at every layer. Rank it above any further 040a behaviour case.
- **The charter's premise does not hold on this environment**: the Skyflow backend module *is* installed, so the failure VCST-4386 was written to fix (type generation without the backend module) cannot be reproduced here at all. It needs an environment without `VirtoCommerce.Skyflow`, or a store with the module setting cleared.
- 040a's 29 cases and 041 all presuppose a loaded module; nothing asserts registration. GAP-10 stands after this session, narrowed to NN-1/NN-2.

## Observations

- Skyflow renders **cart-embedded** on this store (single-step checkout), consistent with 040a and BL-PAY-004 — not the `/checkout/payment` redirect path.
- The `saved-credit-cards` empty state is clean and correctly localized; the module's route registers under `Account`, and its menu item lands in the account **sidebar** (User group), not only the header dropdown.
