## Charter item A — Scenario 20 (P0, load-bearing): SETTLED

Sequence: popover opened once **before** any lock (baseline, both rows enabled, `GetOrganizations` fired) → closed → **V2 lock applied via REST while the page stayed loaded, no reload** → popover reopened.

**A second `GetOrganizations` request fired on reopen** (network req #141, 132–171 ms, same session, no reload) and its response carried `isLockedForCurrentUser:true` for TechFlow; the row rendered `[disabled]` immediately. **Mechanism: the popover component is destroyed and remounted on every open** (not kept alive with a persistent watcher) — `onMounted` firing again is exactly what "refresh on every menu open" produces in practice, even though the PR's own wording ("watch/refresh") and the literal mechanism (remount) are different claims. **Net effect for a user: correct.** Confirmed on **mobile too** — see Charter item C.

## Charter item B — Scenario 22 (P2): partially reached, no race forced

Three rapid clicks (open→close→open, ~1.7 s span) produced exactly one further `GetOrganizations` request (#144/#145 pattern collapsed to one per open, 171 ms then 132 ms, non-overlapping, sequential completions 1 s apart). Final render was consistent with server truth (TechFlow still `[disabled]`) — **no stale repaint observed at normal latency**. `void` discarding the promise with no loading gate is still a real hazard *in principle* (nothing prevents two in-flight requests resolving out of order), but reproducing an actual out-of-order repaint needs artificial latency/throttling this lane cannot apply (no Chrome DevTools network-throttle tool in this session). **NOT REACHED as a reproduced race — reached as "no evidence of it at normal latency."**
