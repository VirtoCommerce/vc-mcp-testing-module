## Risk Areas
- Mobile is the untouched half of this ticket: both account surfaces are fixed at ≥640px only, and the <640px drawer still reproduces the *original* reported defect verbatim.
- VcSelect is shared with checkout/address forms; EXP-03/04/05 are ui-kit-wide, not orders-specific.

## Observations
- Rep trigger raw: `role="button" aria-expanded="false" aria-haspopup="dialog" tabindex="0"` — **no `aria-controls`** (chain L1 names it; APG treats it as optional for dialog triggers).
- Both panels: `role="dialog" tabindex="-1"` + `aria-label`, **no `aria-modal`**, no focus trap — page landmarks stay exposed while open → **non-modal, confirming the static DRIFT**.
- Status checkboxes are native `<input type="checkbox">` carrying a redundant `aria-checked` (consistent, not contradictory).
- Two `role="dialog" aria-label="Calendar"` popovers exist in the DOM on both pages before any calendar is opened.
- Header `[data-test-id="account-button"]` carries `aria-haspopup="true"` (boolean) while the FILTERS trigger carries `aria-haspopup="dialog"` (role token) — the PR's "haspopup derived from role" contract has not reached every consumer. Relevant to S16/S19; the rest of that sweep was not reached.
- Console: only `ws://localhost/graphql` handshake 400s — local nginx does not proxy websockets; env noise, not product.
- One transient "server is currently experiencing technical issues" toast right after a viewport resize on `/account/orders`; the Filters control briefly vanished and returned on reload. Not reproduced; recorded, not claimed.
