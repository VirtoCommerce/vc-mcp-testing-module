## Net-New Scenarios Discovered

| # | Scenario | Oracle ref | Fate |
|---|---|---|---|
| **N1** | **The date badge and the status dot are ONE slot with two semantics, and completion silently wins.** On an in-progress card the slot reads `● 170 days left` (dot = date severity, text = time). On completion the same slot reads `● Mission completed` (dot = status, text = status) and **the deadline disappears entirely** — a mission that is both completed *and* still time-boxed shows no end date anywhere on the card. VCST-5910 (date bands) and VCST-5911 (status dot + open-ended labelling) both write to this slot. The inverse case is the 5911 bug's own shape: an **in-progress open-ended** mission has no time text to show, so only the dot is left. | **NONE** — MSNF-050 asserts severity+completion are not conveyed by colour alone, MSNF-020 asserts a no-end-date mission renders no empty badge shell; neither covers the two writers colliding in one slot. | **PROMOTE → suite 083c** (Frontend/loyalty). Two cases. (a) A **Completed** mission whose `endDate` is still in the future: assert the card and its modal still expose the end date or a completion date somewhere, and that the badge slot's accessible text distinguishes "completed" from "expired". (b) An **in-progress open-ended** mission (no `endDate`): assert its status is conveyed by a text label or a non-colour affordance, not by the dot alone, in **both** colour modes. Fixture for (b) must be seeded in-progress — the existing `MSN_OPEN_ENDED` is Completed and cannot answer it. |
| **N2** | **At 375 px the PerSku modal truncates BOTH product titles to the same visible prefix, and both steppers carry the identical accessible name `Product quantity`.** Observed on `AGENT-TEST-MSN-PROGRESS-COMPLETED`: row 1 and row 2 both read `AGENT-TEST Missions PerSku Targ…`; the only surviving disambiguator is the SKU line (`…-TARGET-B` / `…-TARGET-A`). VCST-5831's truncation removes the *visual* disambiguator exactly where VCST-5912's corrected spinbutton supplies no *programmatic* one — so a user navigating by form controls, or a sighted user at mobile width, cannot attribute a stepper to a target. Neither fix is wrong alone. | **NONE** — MSNF-083 asserts the row reflows and owns its stepper (it does); MSNF-046 covers the *open-mission* control's accessible name. The stepper's accessible name is unasserted anywhere in 083c. | **PROMOTE → suite 083c.** Open a PerSku modal with ≥2 targets at 375 px: assert each quantity spinbutton's accessible name resolves to its own product or SKU (not a shared generic string), **and** that when two titles truncate to the same visible text the row still carries a per-row distinguishing label above the fold. ## Oracle Feedback

- **MSNF-084 is still RED, and for a different reason than the suite records.** The case is held at Draft "blocked on VCST-5910, never promoted until the third band ships". VCST-5910 **has** shipped and **is** deployed — but the band it delivered is keyed on *completion*, not on *window length*. The deployed `resolveDateSeverity()` at `1f40b001` returns `success` when completed, `danger` below `DATE_DANGER_DAYS`, and **`warning` for everything else** — so a mission 170 days out and a mission 11 days out render the identical amber dot, which is precisely what MSNF-084 asserts must not happen. Confirmed live: every in-progress card on page 4 shows amber at `170 days left`. **Do not promote MSNF-084 on the strength of "VCST-5910 is Done"** — it would go straight to a false red. Either the case's premise or the fix's scope needs a product decision.
- No `BL-LOY-*` or `BL-UI-*` invariant was contradicted this session. N1 is a candidate for a new UI invariant — *"a badge slot that encodes two independent dimensions must not let one destroy the other"* — routed to `/qa-review-oracles`, not written here.

## Risk Areas

- **One component tree, one badge slot, two tickets.** N1 is the structural risk the charter predicted: 5910 and 5911 each changed the same element for different reasons and neither ticket's AC mentions the other's dimension.
- **Accessible names are generic where the visual layer is doing the disambiguating.** N2 generalises beyond missions — any modal that truncates at mobile width and reuses one control label has the same shape.
- **Fixture coverage is skewed to `Completed`.** Three of the four charter scenarios need an *in-progress* mission of a specific shape (order-value + live end date; open-ended + live). The account has neither. Any future missions session will hit this wall again.

## Questions

1. **The preset read is indirect and the charter asked for the authoritative one.** `GET /api/white-labeling/store/B2B-store` returned **401** for every token mintable this session (admin password grant on `BACK_URL`; the storefront host serves the SPA shell on that path). Both preset readings above are therefore from the *rendered* primary token, the same basis EXP-01 used — adequate to say "Red, and unchanged across the session", not adequate to name the stored preset record. Who holds a principal that can read that endpoint?
2. Was VCST-5910's third band *intended* to be completion-vs-live rather than long-window-vs-near? MSNF-084 was authored expecting the latter. This needs the ticket's AC, not an inference.
3. Should a **Completed but still time-boxed** mission surface its deadline at all (N1a)? Arguably no — but the card currently makes that choice implicitly, by overwriting.
