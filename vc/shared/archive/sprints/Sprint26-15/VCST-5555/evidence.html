Notes carried with the verdict (none blocking)

  1. P3 — VcTooltip does not forward triggerProps to the #trigger slot. Non-interactive triggers (vc-checkbox__label, vc-input-details__message) therefore carry no role or state. On storefront /search, of 52 .vc-popover__trigger: 4 carry ARIA, 45 have a focusable child but no aria-haspopup/aria-expanded, 3 have no focusable child at all. Keyboard inoperability is pre-existing — emitTriggerProps on dev never contained a tabindex, so role="button" alone never made those triggers focusable. The only new delta is loss of AT role/state announcement on a control that was already unreachable. Recommended as a separate follow-up.
  2. P3 — VcButton focus ring is low-contrast. 3px red at 30 % alpha ≈ 1.63:1 vs white / 2.82:1 vs the button fill, below the 3:1 usually applied under 1.4.11. Pre-existing, untouched by this PR, and invisible to axe (axe does not test focus-indicator contrast).
  3. P3 — VcScrollbar regions have no accessible name. An unnamed Tab stop. Not an AA failure, but the tab stop itself is new, so the unnamed stop is new.
  4. P3 — other presets still fail AA on solid-accent: purple-pink 3.58:1, watermelon 3.14:1. Pre-existing and out of scope — this ticket is Red-scoped.
