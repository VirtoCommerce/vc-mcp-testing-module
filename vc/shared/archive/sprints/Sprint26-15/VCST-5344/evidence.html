The bug & the fix

The shared bank-card-form.vue validated the CVV with a brand-agnostic rule (min(3).max(4), mask "####", static maxlength=4 / placeholder="111") and did no card-brand detection. A 3-digit CVV on Amex (needs 4) and a 4-digit CVV on Visa (needs 3) both passed client validation and enabled Place order; the malformed CVV was only caught downstream by Accept.js, after the VCST-5162 cart flow had already pre-created an unpaid ghost order. The fix (new cvv-validation.ts core + brand-conditional yup rule/mask/maxlength/placeholder) derives the brand from the IIN (Amex 34/37 → 4-digit CVV, else 3).
