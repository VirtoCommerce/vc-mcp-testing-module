## 3. What the unit suite does and does NOT guard  (see red-b-wiring-UNGUARDED.txt)
The fix has three parts. Two are guarded, one is not:

  part                                              file                          guarded?
  resolveShortcutTargetBlade (pure resolver)        focus-target.ts               YES — 5 tests
  data-blade-id marker on the blade root            vc-blade-slot.vue             YES — 1 test

Reverting only the seam (one call) leaves the whole suite green at 179/179 while
reintroducing the reported defect. The unit suite therefore cannot detect a regression of
the user-visible fix; the live check is the only evidence that it works end to end.
